Match Camera

Take the shot you want. Hand over the phone. It guides whoever holds it next back to the exact same angle.

What it is

A native iPhone camera with real manual controls: physical lens switching, ISO, shutter speed, white balance, focus, exposure compensation, RAW and ProRAW, grids, level and histogram. And a Match mode for the photo you cannot afford to get wrong: your first shot becomes a ghost over the viewfinder, the phone remembers the angle it was held at, and whoever takes the next photo gets one instruction at a time, an aim ring, and haptics that tell them when it lines up.

Pricing

The camera is free. Match mode includes two free matched photos, after which it unlocks with a 7-day Week Pass or a one-time Lifetime purchase. The Week Pass does not renew. Purchases are handled by Apple and can be restored on any device signed in to the same Apple Account.
